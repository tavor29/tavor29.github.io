A worked example from the Citizen Development Governance Model, **illustrative, set in a fictional company**. Reuse the columns for your own list; the value is in writing the sequencing recommendation, not just the scores.

## Matrix

| Initiative | Impact | Effort | Confidence | Strategic fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Automated code-review assistant | High | Medium | High | High |
| Internal knowledge-base chatbot | Medium | Low | High | Medium |
| Computer-vision QA tool for hardware testing | High | High | Medium | High |
| Exploratory agent framework for R&D scripting | Unknown | High | Low | Medium |

## Recommendation

Prioritize the code-review assistant first, high impact, high confidence, manageable effort. Keep the computer-vision QA tool on the roadmap but staged behind it, given the higher effort and only medium confidence. Treat the exploratory agent framework as a small, time-boxed research spike rather than a committed initiative until confidence improves.
